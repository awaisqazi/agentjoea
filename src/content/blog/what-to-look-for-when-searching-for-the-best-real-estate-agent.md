If you're in the market for a real estate agent, whether for selling your home, buying a new one, or venturing into investment properties, the big questions loom: Who is the best agent to assist you? What criteria should you prioritize in your search? And how can you identify THE best agent?

While claiming to be the absolute "best" is ambitious, not all agents are indeed alike. The task of finding the ideal person might seem daunting and nearly impossible.

Luckily I have created a checklist to guide you. The best real estate agent must be:

**1) Passionate** — They live and breathe real estate. It's not just a job; it's a lifestyle! Someone who is naturally tuned in and locked into the market, staying up to date daily on all news and trends. Someone who enjoys the industry and can have fun while working hard!

**2) Organized** — A great agent is on top of appointments, returns calls, and is super-efficient and fast when it comes to finding great deals. They have a reliable system in place to handle the entire buying/selling process; from searching for deals to scheduling appointments, making offers, negotiating, and closing.

**3) Networked** — An agent should have more to offer than MLS access. Someone with contacts for off-listing properties, contacts who have great deals in their pockets, contractors who can fix anything at an affordable price, attorneys who can solve any problems, accountants who can assist in tax preparations, and lenders who can help with any financial situation.

**4) Communicative** — Time is of the essence, and there is no time to waste in real estate. You need someone who is responsive!

**5) Client-focused** — They understand your needs above and beyond the type of property they're looking for. An agent needs to understand and care about the client's goals and motivations.

**6) Investment-savvy** — Someone who is in the game! Someone who does more than talk about investments but is actually investing themselves! They know what to look for, what pitfalls to avoid, and where to capitalize.

**7) Experienced** — Someone who has years of dealing with properties, the process, what to look for... the ins and outs.
