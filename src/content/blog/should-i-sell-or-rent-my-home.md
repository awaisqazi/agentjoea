When deciding whether to sell or rent your home, it's important to take into account a variety of factors such as your current financial goals and personal circumstances. Selling your home can provide you with a lump sum of cash, which may be beneficial for other investments or financial needs. However, it's important to keep in mind that renting your property can generate an ongoing income stream, though it does come with added responsibilities such as property management and potential maintenance costs.

When making this decision, it's crucial to consider market conditions, the location of your property, your long-term plans, and your overall financial stability. If you're in need of immediate liquidity or are planning on moving far away from your current location, selling your home may be the most prudent option. However, if you're able to comfortably take on the role of a landlord and believe in the long-term appreciation of your property, renting your home could provide you with stable income and potential tax benefits.
