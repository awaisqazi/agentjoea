It's tough trying to foretell the real estate market. However, there are always opportunities to be had in any market. Yes, interest rates are high and inventory is low. But because of these conditions there are also less buyers and competition as well.

I always ask my clients what are their family/life goals in the next 3–5 years. Does buying a new home or investment property align with these goals? If it does, then don't wait — if you wait for all things to line up perfectly you could be waiting a very long time.
